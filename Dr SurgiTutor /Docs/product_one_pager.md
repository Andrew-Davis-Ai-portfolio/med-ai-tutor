# Dr. SurgiTutor — One Pager

## Product Summary
Dr. SurgiTutor is an **educational AI simulation framework** designed to support
clinical judgment awareness, ethical reasoning, and decision-context training.

It is **not** a diagnostic or treatment system.

## Target Users
- Medical students (pre-clinical)
- Nursing and allied health trainees
- Clinical educators
- Healthcare innovation teams
- Ethics and governance reviewers

## Core Value
Modern healthcare failures often stem from **judgment breakdowns**, not lack of information.

Dr. SurgiTutor focuses on:
- Context recognition
- Risk awareness
- Ethical constraint modeling
- Decision framing under uncertainty

## What It Is
- Scenario-based reasoning simulations
- Non-procedural
- Non-instructional
- Academically discussable
- Enterprise-safe

## What It Is Not
- A medical device
- A replacement for clinicians
- A training certification tool
- An emergency response system

## Deployment Status
Conceptual / Educational demonstration  
Not deployed in clinical environments

> Built to show how AI *should* be constrained — not unleashed.
