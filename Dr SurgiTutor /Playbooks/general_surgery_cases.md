# General Surgery — Educational Case Playbook

> ⚠️ Educational simulation only.  
> Not for diagnosis, treatment, or clinical decision-making.

This playbook provides structured, hypothetical case scenarios used to
demonstrate clinical reasoning, workflow awareness, and AI-supported
education.

---

## Case 1 — Acute Appendicitis (Uncomplicated)

**Patient Profile**
- Age: 24
- Presentation: Right lower quadrant pain, nausea

**Key Considerations**
- Differential diagnosis
- Imaging selection (CT vs ultrasound)
- Pre-op antibiotics
- Timing of surgery

**Teaching Focus**
- Decision thresholds
- Risk of delay
- Clear handoff documentation

---

## Case 2 — Incarcerated Inguinal Hernia

**Patient Profile**
