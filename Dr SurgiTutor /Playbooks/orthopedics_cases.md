# Orthopedics — Educational Case Playbook

> ⚠️ Educational simulation only.

Focus: workflow knowledge, risk awareness, and reasoning—not diagnosis.

---

## Case 1 — Hip Fracture in Elderly Patient

**Patient Profile**
- Age: 79
- Mechanism: Mechanical fall

**Key Considerations**
- Surgical timing
- DVT prevention
- Post-op mobility

**Teaching Focus**
- Coordination of care
- Discharge planning

---

## Case 2 — ACL Tear (Sports Injury)

**Patient Profile**
- Age: 22
- Mechanism: Non-contact injury

**Key Considerations**
- Imaging strategy
- Surgical vs conservative management

**Teaching Focus**
- Patient expectations
- Rehab timelines
