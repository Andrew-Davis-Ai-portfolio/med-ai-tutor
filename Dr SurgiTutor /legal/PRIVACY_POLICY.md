# Privacy Policy

Dr. SurgiTutor does not collect, store, or process personal health information (PHI).

This repository:
- Operates as static documentation and simulation design
- Does not include user accounts
- Does not log personal data
- Does not transmit identifiable information

If deployed in the future with interactive components, privacy considerations will be updated accordingly.

Until such time:
- No user data is retained
- No tracking mechanisms are present
- No analytics are collected

Privacy by design is a core principle of this project.
