# med-ai-tutor
Medical Roleplay Tutor for clinical training simulations, ethical decision-making scenarios, and healthcare workflow automation. Part of the Flame Integration Framework.
---

## 🩺 HOLO-NURSE — Visual Prototype (V1)

**HOLO-NURSE** is the holographic nursing assistant component of the  
**Dr. P.AI / Med-AI-Tutor ecosystem**, designed for:

- Clinical training simulations
- Nursing education & onboarding
- Workflow walkthroughs (non-diagnostic)
- Patient communication modeling (educational only)

> ⚠️ Educational use only — not for diagnosis or treatment.
